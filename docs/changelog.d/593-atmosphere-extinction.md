**`atmosphere.Atmosphere.Extinction` gives a site's extinction coefficient from its own air**: Rayleigh scattering from the surface pressure, ozone from its column, and aerosol from its optical depth. It reproduces Paranal's measured extinction curve within 0.01 mag/airmass, and Mauna Kea's published decomposition term by term.
