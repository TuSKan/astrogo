**The η-Aquariids and Orionids are now checked against observed returns.** Against Egal et al.'s (2020) re-analysis of IMO's visual data for 2001–2019, each shower's maximum falls 0.10° from the median return, its rate is within twice the returns' scatter, and its activity profile is within ×1.8 of the observed average down to a tenth of the maximum.
