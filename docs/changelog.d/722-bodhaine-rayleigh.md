**`atmosphere.RayleighOpticalDepth` follows the Rayleigh exponent into the blue.** It is Bodhaine et al. (1999) Eq. 30, within 0.05% of their exact Table 3 from 250 to 1000 nm. The single λ^−4.05 power law it replaces read 4.2% low at 330 nm. Moonlight, skyglow and extinction all move together: the default V extinction is 0.161 mag/airmass at sea level and 0.131 at Paranal.
