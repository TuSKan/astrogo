**`plan.WithHorizonRefraction` refracts a site's rise and set horizon through the air it names**, in place of the almanacs' fixed 34′: 33.85′ at 10 °C and 1010 hPa, 38.6′ at −20 °C and 1030 hPa. The default stays 34′. No model predicts a real rise or set better than about 2 minutes, and the docs say so.
