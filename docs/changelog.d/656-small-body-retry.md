**`catalog/jpl` resolved "Eros" to Pluto's moon Kerberos**, "Hebe" to Jupiter's Thebe and "Iris" to OSIRIS-REx, because Horizons searches its major bodies first and matches a name inside another. When no target carries the query as a whole word, `Search` now asks Horizons' small bodies with a trailing semicolon, and keeps that answer when it has one.
