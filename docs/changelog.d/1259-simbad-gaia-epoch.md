**`catalog/simbad` reports a star whose position SIMBAD took from Gaia at Gaia's epoch**, where Gaia measured it. SIMBAD moves such a position to J2000.0 without the radial velocity and keeps Gaia's proper motion as it is. Read as a star at J2000.0, Barnard's star landed 164 mas from its Gaia DR3 row; it now lands within Gaia's stated error.
