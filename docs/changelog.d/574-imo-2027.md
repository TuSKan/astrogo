**The meteor shower table follows IMO's 2027 Working List.** The Southern δ-Aquariids peaked three days early (λ☉ 125° instead of 128°), several ZHRs, population indices and radiants were out of date, every maximum was rounded to the whole degree (17 h for the Ursids), and activity windows were short of IMO's dates. Every value now comes from the IMO Meteor Shower Calendar 2027, and each activity window is the Sun's J2000 longitude on IMO's dates.
