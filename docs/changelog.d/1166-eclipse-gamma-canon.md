`plan.EclipseEvent.Gamma` is the gamma NASA's Five Millennium Canons publish: signed, positive north, in Earth equatorial radii. It was the distance as a fraction of the grazing one, unsigned, and read 0.2239 for the 2024 April 8 eclipse whose gamma is 0.3431. It agrees with all 2885 canon eclipses over six centuries to 0.0002.
