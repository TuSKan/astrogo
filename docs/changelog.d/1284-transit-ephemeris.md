**`plan.TransitEphemeris` and `plan.ObserveTransits` predict exoplanet and eclipsing-binary transits and what a site can observe of each.** From a BJD_TDB ephemeris with its uncertainty and epoch–period correlation they give the cycles whose windows reach into a range, each moved to the site's clock, and which of its window, baselines and a k·σ margin included, the constraints leave observable, with the reason each lost stretch is lost.
