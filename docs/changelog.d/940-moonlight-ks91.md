**Scattered moonlight is measured against a published reference.** The reference is the twelve moonlit-sky measurements of Krisciunas & Schaefer (1991) Table 1, which take the place of an uncited "~18 mag/arcsec²" that a test accepted anywhere from 16.5 to 20.5. On the CALSPEC solar spectrum the component reads 0.30 mag faint on average, which is outside its uncertainty budget. The documents now say so, and the cause is tracked separately.
