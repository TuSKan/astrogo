**`skybrightness.ScatteredMoonlight` placed the Moon from the Earth's center**, up to its horizontal parallax, about 0.95°, above where the site sees it: a Moon Skyfield puts at 9.43° was taken at 10.39° and charged too little air, and one just below the site's horizon lit the sky. It now places the Moon, and takes its phase angle, as the site sees it. Observatory's moonlight moves by 0.7 to 1.9 per cent.
