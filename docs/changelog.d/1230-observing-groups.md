**`plan.VisibleTonight` lists each point of light once:** stars closer together than the observer resolves, one minute of arc by default or `plan.WithResolution`, are one row with each star in `plan.VisibleObject.Members`, at their combined magnitude. `resolve.Target.MemberOf`, which SIMBAD's bright results fill from its hierarchy, keeps a star system's light from being added to its components'.
